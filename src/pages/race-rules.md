One of the great aspect of outrigger paddling is racing. Racing gives everyone the opportunity to experience competition in a supportive environment. Races are held throughout the season.

Sprint races are typically held over 500 – 2000 meters. Iron distance races typically are held between 9 –18 miles, while change-out races can be as long as 40 miles.

**See the official PNWORCA race rules and documentation below.**

*Questions?* **Contact us.**
