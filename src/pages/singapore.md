## 2026 will see the IVF World Sprint Championships taking place in Asia for the first time!

The [**Va’a Association Singapore**](https://www.facebook.com/vaa.association.sg?__tn__=-%5dK*F) will host the 2026 [**International Va’a Federation**](https://www.facebook.com/InternationalVaaFederation?__tn__=-%5dK*F) World Sprint Championships, August 17-31 in Singapore. The V6 canoes are expected to be [**Kai Wa’a Hydra**](https://www.kaiwaa.com/hydra-oc6). The V1 canoe is between Fai 4Si and ARE designs.

All U.S. competitors must attend the U.S. Sprint National Championship as the qualifier.  The event is being hosted by the **[Pacific Northwest Outrigger Racing Canoe Association](/)**(PNWORCA) on **Oct. 4-5, 2025** The US Nationals race site has been moved from Vancouver Lake Regional Park, Vancouver, WA to the Oregon side of the Columbia River at **Blue Lake Regional Park in Fairview, OR**. Blue Lake Regional Park is a fresh water, spring fed lake great for viewing the races and racing..   Planning is in the works, stay tuned and save the dates!

*Gather your club members, V1 paddlers and crews to attend the 2025 US National Sprint Championships!*
