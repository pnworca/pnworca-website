This page explains what information the PNWORCA website collects and what we do with it. Last updated November 2026.

## What we collect

**Forms.** When you send a contact form, a nomination or a grant application, we receive what you type into the form, such as your name, email address, club and message. We use it only to respond to you or to handle that nomination or application.

**Spam checks.** Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated spam. Turnstile checks your browser when you use a form. Cloudflare's handling of that data is covered by the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**Hosting.** The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and protect the site.

We do not use advertising, tracking cookies or third-party analytics, and we do not sell or share your information for marketing.

## How long we keep it

Form entries are stored in a PNWORCA account and deleted automatically after 90 days. Messages forwarded to board members by email stay in their inboxes as normal correspondence.

## Links to other sites

Race, club and resource pages link to sites run by clubs, race hosts and other organizations. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

## Contact

To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, [contact us](/contact/).
